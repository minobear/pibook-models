Modified by Pibook: the model was converted to TensorFlow Lite and Core ML; the FFT was replaced
by equivalent DFT matrix multiplications; BatchNorm layers were folded into convolutions;
transposed convolutions were rewritten as equivalent polyphase convolutions; reflect padding
is expressed as MIRROR_PAD; convolution weights are stored as 8-bit integers (three small
layers and the Fourier units stay in 32-bit float).
